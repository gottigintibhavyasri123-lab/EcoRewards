# EcoRewards

**Do good for the planet. Get rewarded for your impact.**

EcoRewards is a reward-based street waste management platform. People understand the value of a clean environment, but joining community cleanups is hard to keep up. EcoRewards gives them a reason to participate.

## How it works
1. Upload a photo of dumped waste.
2. Clean suitable litter safely (small, dry waste only, with gloves).
3. Submit an after photo for verification.
4. Once approved, earn **EcoPoints** (+10 per approved cleanup).
5. Redeem EcoPoints for shopping coupons and partner rewards (first coupon at 50 points).

## Prototype features
- Welcome screen and brown-and-white visual identity
- Photo-based cleanup flow with simulated verification (location, time, before/after check, volunteer review)
- Safety guidance for hazardous waste
- Points system with progress to the first coupon
- Partner rewards and coupon redemption
- 24 languages in the picker; full interface translation in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Spanish, French and Arabic (right-to-left supported)
- Nearby municipal offices with map, filters, hours and phone numbers

## Important
This is a front-end prototype. Verification, offices, partners, coupons and points use **sample data**. A real version needs a backend, GPS and image verification, and a municipal directory.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step or dependencies.
