Project overview
EcoRewards makes street cleanup easier to keep doing by paying people back in everyday rewards. People value a clean environment, but joining community cleanups is hard to sustain. EcoRewards gives them a reason to take part and a way to see the difference they make.
Tagline: Do good for the planet. Get rewarded for your impact.

SOP for citizens
A citizen either cleans a spot and submits proof, or reports it to the municipal office. Both routes follow the steps below.
Safety rules (apply to every cleanup)
• Clean only small, dry litter, and wear gloves.
• Never touch glass, needles, medical waste or chemical waste. Report those to the office instead.
• Stay away from traffic and do not lift heavy items.
Procedure A: clean it yourself
1. Choose a dumping spot on the Home tab, or open Clean and select "I'll clean it".
2. Take a before photo of the waste at the spot.
3. Tick the gloves confirmation. The after-photo box unlocks.
4. Clean the litter and put it in the nearest bin or collection point.
5. Take an after photo from the same place and angle.
6. Submit for verification. The report gets an ID (for example ER-3002) and shows "Pending review".
7. Wait for the office decision. If approved, you earn +10 EcoPoints. If rejected, you see the reason and can submit again.
Procedure B: report to the municipal office
1. Open Clean and select "Report to municipal office".
2. Photograph the dumped waste and press "Send to municipal office".
3. Note the report ID (for example ER-1041). Only one open report is allowed per spot.
4. Follow the status: Pending review, Approved by office, Cleanup in progress, Cleaned and confirmed.
5. You get a notification when the cleanup is confirmed.
In the prototype you can upload a photo or use a sample photo. The full app is planned to require live camera capture.
